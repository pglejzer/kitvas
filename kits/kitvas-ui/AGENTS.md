# kits/kitvas-ui

The built-in kit that carries the studio's own look: its tokens in eight themes, its components, its 62 icons and its typeface. It is the only built-in kit and the default of a project (ADR 0090). Add no colour, shadow, gradient or icon package the studio does not have.

- `kit.json` is the catalog agents read. A component, prop, value or icon exists for agents only when it is listed there. Change the source and the catalog together.
- One component per file in `tsx/<category>/`, where the folder is the `category` of its catalog entry (`action`, `input`, `layout`, `typography`, `display`, `navigation`, `overlay`, `feedback`). Shared helpers live in `tsx/core/`. Only `index.ts`, which exports every component, sits directly in `tsx/`.
- Function components, `{...rest}` forwarded: layout and display components to the root element, field components to the form control. Classes are `kit-<component>`, `kit-<component>--<value>` and `kit-<component>__<part>`. Sizes are `sm` (28 px, the default), `md` (36) and `lg` (44).
- Styles in `scss/<category>/`, one partial per component group, listed in `scss/index.scss`; the mixins (`focus-ring`, `disabled`, `control`, `hit`, `boundary`, `on-panel`, `on-raised`, `pop`), the page rules, the keyframes and the typeface in `scss/core/`. The typeface partial `_font.scss` is imported by `tsx/index.ts`, not by the sheet: only code the bundler reads may point at a file with `url()`. Use the `--kit-*` names of `json/tokens.json`, the locals `--_shadow-*` and `--_ease-out` of `_studio.scss`, and a component's own locals that start with `--_`. Never a raw colour.
- Every control has rest, hover, focus, pressed and disabled looks, and a field an invalid one. A hover only changes colour; it never hides or weakens the text (`reference/controls.md`).
- Colour pairs. A component may sit on any ground (`canvas`, `panel`, `raised`, `pop`) in any of the eight themes, so it uses only pairs that read on all of them:
  - Text is `ink`. Quiet text is `inkMid`, on a ground or on `raised`, `raisedStrong` or `pressed`, never on a soft fill.
  - On a soft fill (`accentSoft`, `fixSoft`, `reviewSoft`, `goodSoft`) the text is `ink`. On `accent` it is `onAccent`; on `fix`, `review` or `good` as a solid fill it is `onTone`.
  - `fix` and `good` may colour text on a ground only (an error note, a danger button at rest), never on a fill. When a hover brings a soft fill under such text, the text turns `ink`.
  - `accent` and `review` never colour text, and `inkSoft` never colours text or a mark that carries meaning: on a ground other than the panel they fall under 4.5 to 1. The accent and the tones show as a fill, a ring, an underline, a bar, a dot or an icon, where 3 to 1 is enough.
  - What is chosen or on (a tab, a nav item, a segment, a pressed pill, a checked toggle) carries a mark that reads 3 to 1 against what is beside it: a border or a bar of `accent`, or the solid `accent` fill of a checked box or switch with its sign in `onAccent`; a chosen radio keeps its fill and takes an `accent` ring around an `accent` dot. A step of fill alone is too weak to be that mark. Its fill is `accentSoft` or the next step, its text `ink`. A link is `ink` text with an underline of `accent`.
- Steps. A control's quiet fill sits one step above the ground it is on, as in the studio: a control never names `raised`, `raisedStrong` or `pressed` for its fill, it uses `mixins.quiet()` (a field, a chip, a soft button, a track) and `mixins.quiet-strong()` (a neutral solid button, what is chosen in a track). A surface sets the two for what it holds: `mixins.on-panel` when its ground is `panel`, `mixins.on-raised` when it is `raised` or `pop` (the `pop` mixin has it). Without a surface the page's ground gives `raised` and `pressed`.
- Edges. A field and a toggle (a checkbox, a radio, a switch) have a real border from `mixins.boundary`, which reads 3 to 1 on every ground. An edge that comes and goes (under the pointer, pressed, chosen) is a border whose colour changes, kept `transparent` at rest. No ring is drawn with `box-shadow`: the checks read only a border as an edge and count every shadow. `box-shadow` is for the elevation locals `--_shadow-*` alone.
- Generated, never edited by hand (`npm run kit-ui-tokens`): `json/tokens.json`, `json/tokens.<theme>.json`, `scss/core/_studio.scss` and `tsx/display/icon-paths.ts`. A new colour or scale step goes into `src/studio/theme/tokens.ts`, or, when it is the kit's own, into the `ADDED` table of `scripts/kitvas-ui-tokens.ts`; then run the script.
- The typeface files in `fonts/` are the ones of `@fontsource-variable/figtree`, with that package's licence beside them. The kit holds no kitvas logo: the name and the logo are reserved (`LICENSING.md`).
- `test/kit/kitvas-ui-kit.test.ts` checks all of the above, including that no file sits loose outside a category folder and that every pair of colours the controls use reads in every theme. Run it after any change here.
